# Classical representatives of the frozen Stage 3 distributions

This analysis concerns the **same distribution in fixed PDE coordinates**,
allowing arbitrary invertible point-dependent changes of its two generators.
It does not infer conditional invariance of the PDE, classify a point
equivalence groupoid, or claim a new solution family. The 64 candidates are
frozen in `nonclassical_candidates.json`, SHA-256
`2409ee048ab8ed66bdf4d0840a34a95b6ea380fdb72c2aedc29ce84382b3b764`.
The module checks the accepted Stage 2 classification gate before making an
ambient absence assertion. No earlier source or receipt is modified.

## 1. The question and the complete classical algebra

On the open point-coordinate chart
`(t,x,y,h,u,v,B)`, with `h>0,u^2+v^2>0`, put

\[
\mathcal D=\operatorname{span}\{Y,Q\},\qquad Y=\partial_y,\quad
Q=\partial_t+c\partial_x+\sum_{i=1}^{4}(a_iU_i+b_i)\partial_{U_i},
\qquad (U_1,U_2,U_3,U_4)=(h,u,v,B).
\]

The constants `c,a_i,b_i` are fixed for each candidate. The distribution has
rank two everywhere because the `y` and `t` projections of `Y,Q` are
independent. Its two displayed generators commute, but this alone says
nothing about whether it is generated by classical symmetries.

For cubic transport with `K>0,G=f=0,g>0`, the accepted unrestricted
classification supplies all classical point fields in the form

\[
\begin{aligned}
\tau&=\alpha t+t_0,&
\xi&=\beta x-\omega y+x_0,&
\zeta&=\omega x+\beta y+y_0,\\
\eta_h&=2(\beta-\alpha)h,&
\eta_u&=(\beta-\alpha)u-\omega v,&
\eta_v&=\omega u+(\beta-\alpha)v,\\
\eta_B&=2(\beta-\alpha)B+b_0.
\end{aligned}
\]

All seven coefficients `(t0,x0,y0,b0,omega,alpha,beta)` are **constants**.
The code obtains this basis from the same solved family used by
`classification_2d.py` and requires its current independent review gate.
It does not replace the complete algebra with a handpicked trial set.

## 2. Tangency of every classical field

A classical field `C` belongs to the candidate distribution precisely when

\[
C=\tau Q+\zeta Y,
\qquad \xi=c\tau,\qquad
\eta_i=\tau(a_iU_i+b_i)\quad(i=1,\ldots,4).
\]

These are polynomial identities on an ambient open set, not equations at a
sampled point or on a prescribed solution graph. The first identity gives

\[
\beta=\omega=0,\qquad c\alpha=0,\qquad x_0=ct_0.
\]

The depth identity is now

\[
-2\alpha h=(\alpha t+t_0)(a_hh+b_h).
\]

Its `th` coefficient gives `alpha*a_h=0`; its `h` coefficient gives
`-2alpha=t0*a_h`. If `a_h!=0`, the first equation gives `alpha=0`; if
`a_h=0`, the second does. Thus `alpha=0` on **every** parameter stratum,
without dividing by `c`, a velocity component, or a candidate coefficient.
The remaining complete constant system is

\[
\begin{gathered}
\alpha=\beta=\omega=0,\qquad x_0=ct_0,\qquad b_0=t_0b_B,\\
t_0a_h=t_0a_u=t_0a_v=t_0a_B=
t_0b_h=t_0b_u=t_0b_v=0,
\qquad y_0\text{ arbitrary}.
\end{gathered}
\]

Consequently a fixed classical rank-two representative exists **if and only if**

\[
\boxed{a_h=a_u=a_v=a_B=b_h=b_u=b_v=0.}
\]

On this stratum the fixed abelian subalgebra is
`span{partial_y, partial_t+c partial_x+b_B partial_B}`. Otherwise the entire
classical tangent space is only `span{partial_y}`, of evaluated rank one.
The implementation independently solves the full polynomial tangency
matrix for each fixed candidate and checks the kernel and its rank against
this theorem. Unknown coefficient zero strata are retained explicitly.

## 3. Why arbitrary changes of frame are included

If a fixed two-dimensional classical subalgebra generated `D` on an open
set, both of its generators would belong to the constant classical tangent
space just computed. A rank-one tangent space cannot generate a rank-two
distribution, regardless of variable coefficients used to recombine its
basis. Conversely, the displayed classical pair is a sufficient witness on
the zero-coefficient stratum. This is stronger than testing whether the
single printed `Q` is a classical generator.

For a nontrivial positive control let `F=(Y,Q)` and

\[
\widetilde F=F M,\qquad
M=\begin{pmatrix}h&t\\0&1/h\end{pmatrix},\qquad \det M=1.
\]

The new frame is `(hY,tY+Q/h)`. Its members generally are not classical
point fields, yet its distribution is the same. On a classical candidate,
the module reconstructs the fixed classical basis as `F_tilde*M^{-1}`.
For the negative candidate `Q=partial_t+u partial_u`, the same variable
frame change leaves the ambient rank-one obstruction unchanged. Additional
frame poles and determinant-zero sets are excluded only by explicit local
frame guards; they are not hidden restrictions of the model classification.

## 4. Solution-level coincidence is a different statement

The ambiently inequivalent candidate `Q=partial_t+u partial_u` restricts to
`partial_t` on the exact uniform graph `(h,u,v,B)=(2,0,1,0)`. Its distribution
there coincides with the classical translation distribution. This graph
has nonzero speed and uses the velocity axis `u=0` legitimately. A single
solution coincidence does not establish ambient equality or the
conditional-invariance criterion. The code verifies this negative control
against the original cubic four-row PDE as well as the restricted frames.

There is a second, distinct phenomenon for the four nonzero pure shifts
`c=0,b_h=+/-1` and `c=0,b_u=+/-1`: their **full local solution families** are
invariant under other fixed classical subalgebras, even though the two
rank-two distributions remain different on those graphs.

For a depth shift `beta=b_h!=0`, the invariant-surface equations give
`h=beta*t+H(x),u=U(x),v=V(x),B=Z(x)`. The coefficient of `t` in mass is
`beta*U'(x)`, hence `U'=0`. Mass then gives `beta+U0 H'=0`, forcing
`U0!=0`; momentum gives `H'+Z'=0` and `U0 V'=0`. Every local smooth solution
graph in this family therefore has the form

\[
h=H_0+\beta t-\frac{\beta x}{U_0},\qquad u=U_0\ne0,\qquad
v=V_0,\qquad B=B_0+\frac{\beta x}{U_0}.
\]

Here `H0` is an arbitrary real intercept; positive depth is imposed on the
local `(t,x)` chart and does not require `H0>0`. On that chart it solves all four original equations and is
invariant under the fixed classical pair
`{partial_y, partial_t+U0 partial_x+beta partial_B}`. The four profile
constants are fixed when this subalgebra is chosen. The candidate and
classical projection planes together have rank three, with witness `U0`,
so this is **containment of solution graphs**, not equality of distributions.

For a longitudinal velocity shift `beta=b_u!=0`, the invariant-surface
equations give `h=H(x),u=beta*t+U(x),v=V(x),B=Z(x)`. The mass coefficient of
`t` forces `H'=0`; positive depth then forces `U'=0`. The transverse
coefficient of `t` forces `V'=0`, and momentum gives `Z'=-beta/g`. Thus

\[
h=H_0>0,\qquad u=U_0+\beta t,\qquad v=V_0,\qquad
B=B_0-\frac{\beta x}{g}.
\]

This exact family is invariant under the fixed classical pair
`{partial_y, partial_x-(beta/g)partial_B}`. Again the combined projection
rank is three. Its containment statement is recorded on the regular
conditional-invariance chart `u!=0`. The explicit solution formula can
extend through `u=0` when `v!=0`; this does not repair the separate
conditional-invariance failure at other singular first jets.

The code verifies original PDE residuals and both candidate and classical
characteristics for these families. These calculations supply no novel
classical-inequivalent solution family.

## 5. Interface, frozen counts, and validation

`classical_distribution_equivalence(candidate, basis_change=None,
require_gate=True)` returns its `status`, the full constant tangency solve,
rank evidence, a classical witness or ambient obstruction, and a separate
`solution_family_containment` result where available.
`evaluate_frozen_registry()` requires both the frozen SHA-256 and its Stage 2
gate fingerprint. It preserves all 64 IDs and their order. The exact
coefficient criterion selects nine classical distributions: `C00-C02` and
`C45-C50`. The remaining 55 have no fixed classical representative on an
ambient open chart. These counts are **not** counts of conditionally
invariant candidates; that independent gate may reject candidates in either
category or restrict their chart.

The test file first failed collection because the new module did not yet
exist. Run the complete bounded validation with

```powershell
python -B -c "import subprocess,sys; subprocess.run([sys.executable,'-B','-m','pytest','build/sve-general-symm-20261020/tests/test_nonclassical_equivalence.py','-q'],timeout=60,check=True)"
```

The final bounded run passed **82 tests in 4.27 seconds** under the enforced
60-second process limit. It covers all 64 frozen candidates, a nontrivial
variable frame change, ambient negative controls, special-solution collapse,
the four classically contained shift families, unknown parameter strata,
missing source review, invalid input/frame rejection, and local depth
positivity without an unjustified intercept restriction. Independent Stage 3
review and release gates remain separate. No Stage 1 or Stage 2 source was edited.
