\block{1\ \ The gap (why now)}{
\large
Machine learning for PDEs is powerful but \textbf{over-claimed}, and classical
semi-analytics is \textbf{under-certified}:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Weak baselines.} McGreivy \& Hakim (\emph{Nat.\ Mach.\ Intell.}\ 2024):
        $\sim$79\% of ML-for-PDE papers beat only weak baselines.
  \item \textbf{Numerics still win.} Grossmann et al.\ (2024): FEM beats PINNs in time
        \emph{and} accuracy by 1--6 orders on standard problems.
  \item \textbf{Symmetry losses can be empty.} Point-symmetry PINN/PINO losses
        ``oftentimes give \emph{no training signal}'' (Akhound-Sadegh, NeurIPS 2023;
        ``Generalized Lie Symmetries in PINOs'', arXiv:2502.00373).
  \item \textbf{HAM convergence is asserted, not proven.} The cleanest theory
        (Turkyilmazoglu 2010) is only \emph{conditional}; Liao lists a-priori
        convergence as open.
\end{itemize}
\vspace{4mm}
\textbf{Opportunity:} fuse Lie \emph{reduction} $+$ semi-analytics $+$ a \emph{small}
ML correction, and ship a \emph{runtime certificate} --- the piece every lineage lacks.
}

\column{0.5}
\block{2\ \ Our direction: CCS-SYM\textsuperscript{+}}{
\large
A reduction-first hybrid whose \textbf{headline is rigorous error control}, not raw accuracy.
Four coupled stages, each carrying the symmetry group as a hard inductive bias:
\vspace{3mm}

\textbf{REDUCE.} Solve the linear determining system
$\mathrm{pr}^{(k)}X(\Delta)\!\mid_{\Delta=0}=0$ for the Lie algebra; reduce the PDE to a
low-dimensional ODE $N[U]=0$ in an invariant variable $z$ (nonclassical / approximate-BGI
fallback where point symmetry is barren).

\textbf{CLOSE.} Homotopy-analysis series $U_M(z;\hbar)=\sum_{m=0}^{M}u_m(z)$ ---
interpretable, data-free.

\textbf{CERTIFY {\normalsize(the novelty)}.} A theorem-backed triple:
(a) ratio $\rho_k=\lVert u_{k+1}\rVert/\lVert u_k\rVert<r<1$ with a geometric-tail bound;
(b) a closedness lemma so convergence $\Rightarrow N[U_\infty]=0$;
(c) a \emph{computable} a-posteriori bound $\lVert U-U_M\rVert\le C\,\lVert N[U_M]\rVert$.

\textbf{CORRECT.} A \emph{tiny} symmetry-invariant network in invariant coordinates,
$u_\theta=\Lambda(z)\,N_\theta(z)+U_M(z;\hbar^\ast)$ --- exactly equivariant, CPU-trained.
}

\textbf{Family (representative)} & \textbf{Core idea \& strength} &

\textbf{Classical numerics} \newline FEM / FDM / spectral &
Discretize on a mesh; mature, gold-standard accuracy, complex geometry, a-posteriori control. &
Curse of dimensionality; per-problem meshing; no closed-form insight; sharp fronts need adaptivity. &
We avoid discretizing the full domain via symmetry: analytic reduced solution $+$ explicit bounds --- when symmetry exists (else FEM is the fallback). \\ \hline

\textbf{PINNs} \newline Raissi et al.\ 2019; XPINN / causal &
NN minimizes PDE residual by autodiff collocation; mesh-free, inverse problems, no data. &
Hard optimization; no general convergence/error guarantee; GPU-heavy; accuracy $\sim10^{-2}$--$10^{-3}$. &
A \emph{tiny} invariant corrector on a \emph{certified} analytic backbone; theorem-backed error; trains on a CPU. \\ \hline

\textbf{Neural operators} \newline FNO (Li 2021), DeepONet (Lu 2021) &
Learn function-space maps for instant amortized many-query inference; resolution-invariant. &
Large data \& compute; weak out-of-distribution extrapolation; no per-instance certificate; opaque. &
Different regime: operators win at amortized high-D fields; we give \emph{single certified} solutions with $\approx$ no data. \\ \hline

\textbf{PDE foundation models} \newline Poseidon (2405.19101), DPOT (ICML 2024), PDEformer-2 (2507.15409) &
Massive multi-physics pretraining; broad few-/zero-shot transfer across PDE families. &
Enormous pretraining cost; unpredictable accuracy; no guarantees; reproducibility/audit concerns. &
Opposite philosophy: \emph{proof-within-a-niche on a laptop} vs.\ breadth-without-proof. \\ \hline

\textbf{Generative / diffusion} \newline DiffusionPDE (2406.17763) &
Score/diffusion over solution fields; UQ, partial observation, stochastic/ill-posed problems. &
Very data/compute-intensive; sampling latency; no deterministic accuracy guarantee; can hallucinate. &
Deterministic and \emph{certified}, not generative; we do not target stochastic field-completion. \\ \hline

\textbf{Symmetry-informed ML} \newline equivariant nets; symmetry-loss PINNs/PINO &
Bake symmetry into architecture/loss; better data efficiency, physics-consistency. &
Symmetry is a \emph{soft prior}, still black-box \& uncertified; many point generators give no signal; no convergence theorem. &
\textbf{Nearest neighbor:} we use symmetry to \emph{analytically reduce} (not just regularize) and \emph{certify}; ML is a bounded corrector. \\ \hline

\textbf{Plain semi-analytical} \newline HAM (Liao); ADM / VIM / DTM &
Closed-form series; interpretable, cheap, no data. &
Convergence \emph{assumed} not proven; far-field / strong-nonlinearity degradation; hand-tuned parameters. &
Semi-analytics done \emph{rigorously}: principled Lie reduction $+$ convergence/error \emph{theorems} $+$ a learned range extension. \\ \hline

\block{4\ \ Already runs --- verified on a laptop CPU}{
\large
\texttt{sympy 1.14 / numpy / scipy / torch 2.12+cpu}; seconds; no GPU. Every claim re-executed.
\vspace{2mm}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Burgers} $u_t+uu_x=\nu u_{xx}$: 5-generator algebra, invariance residual
        $\equiv0$; travelling wave $\to$ exact $\tanh$ shock (residual 0).
  \item \textbf{Porous medium} $u_t=(u^mu_x)_x$: scaling $a=2b-me$; Barenblatt self-similar
        profile verified for $m=1,2,3$.
  \item \textbf{Fisher--KPP} (non-integrable front): a \emph{genuine} HAM deformation series
        with an \textbf{$\hbar$-dependent} Turkyilmazoglu certificate --- diverges at
        $\hbar=-1$, \emph{certifies} at $\hbar=-0.3,-0.6$; order-wise rel-$L^2$
        $1.0\!\times\!10^{-1}\!\to\!4.5\!\times\!10^{-2}\!\to\!2.0\!\times\!10^{-2}$.
  \item \textbf{ML corrector (S4):} a \textbf{321-parameter} MLP, \textbf{0.13\,s} CPU,
        \textbf{rel-$L^2=8.7\!\times\!10^{-4}$} vs.\ the exact Ablowitz--Zeppetella front.
  \item \textbf{$\hbar^\ast$ selection} recovers the exact value to $1.7\!\times\!10^{-7}$.
\end{itemize}
$\Rightarrow$ the convergence certificate is a \emph{real, falsifiable} object, not a tautology.
}

\column{0.48}
\block{5\ \ Honest scope}{
\large
\textbf{Where we win}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Symmetry-rich PDEs with self-similar / invariant structure (scaling, blow-up, fronts).
  \item Trust-critical settings: certified error bounds, not just empirical accuracy.
  \item Low-resource / reproducible: laptop CPU, zero datasets, fully auditable.
  \item Interpretability-first: a closed-form reduced solution beats a black box.
  \item Sample-starved problems where data-hungry models cannot be trained.
\end{itemize}
\vspace{2mm}
\textbf{Where we do \emph{not} compete} (and say so)
\begin{itemize}
  \item High-D PDEs with \emph{no} exploitable symmetry --- nothing to collapse.
  \item Full-field operator learning / amortized many-query --- FNO/DeepONet own this.
  \item Arbitrary geometry / general BCs --- classical FEM/FVM remain right.
  \item Stochastic, ill-posed, field-completion / UQ-by-sampling --- diffusion models.
  \item Broad cross-family transfer --- the foundation-model regime.
\end{itemize}
}
