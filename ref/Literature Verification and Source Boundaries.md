# Literature verification and source boundaries

Checked during the 23 November 2026 audit. AlphaXiv supplied extracted source text for the preprints below. The two journal articles that define the Eulerian and printed scalar reproduction targets were also checked directly against their publisher pages and the project PDFs. External papers establish their own model assumptions. The dissertation's new results are checked by its proofs and replay.

| Source examined | What transfers to the manuscript | Boundary retained |
|---|---|---|
| [Lie Symmetry Analysis of the One-Dimensional Saint-Venant-Exner Model](https://www.mdpi.com/2073-8994/14/8/1679), §2–4 and Eq. (25) | Source of the Eulerian shallow-water balances, power-law closure and claimed algebra/optimal system. | Algebra and printed cubic reduction are independently checked on the declared positive transport chart. |
| [Lie Symmetries and Similarity Solutions for a Shallow-Water Model with Bed Elevation in Lagrange Variables](https://www.mdpi.com/2227-7390/14/3/433), Eq. (7), Proposition 1 and §3 | Defines printed \(P\) and nine reduction entries. | No source claim certifies the compatible material \(M\); the held \(P\)–\(M\) comparison is future work. The maintained reproduction hashes the bundled v3 source PDF. |
| [Symmetries and Exact Solutions of the Rotating Shallow Water Equations](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/0808.1490v1), Eq. (1.1) and §2 | Flat-bottom rotating shallow-water analogy. | Its nine generators are for a three-field system without the evolving sediment balance. The annular SVE calculation has its own affine search. |
| [Lie Symmetry Analysis of the Saint–Venant Equations with Variable Bottom](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2610.34129v1), Eq. (1.1) | Classification with prescribed bottom topography. | A prescribed bottom is part of the coefficients; the dissertation uses an evolving bed. |
| [Contributions to the Algorithmic Foundations of Approximate Lie Symmetry Algebras of Differential Equations](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2410.20608v1), abstract and introduction | Motivation for stating elimination and parameter domains precisely. | The project derives its own determining identities and states where the calculation is restricted. |
| [Invariant Reduction for Partial Differential Equations. I: Conservation Laws and Systems with Two Independent Variables](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2412.02965v1) and [Invariant Variation Problems](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/physics/0503066v3) | Invariant conservation laws and variational symmetry illustrate conditions that can yield first integrals. | The manuscript obtains the material first integral by a direct identity. Its Lie generators do not by themselves establish conservation laws. |
| [Systems of Briot-Bouquet Equations with Analytic Solutions](https://doi.org/10.4153/CMB-1982-032-0) | Classical analytic singular ODE setting. | The local annular two-branch statement follows from the manuscript's saddle and contraction argument. |
| [A Hyperbolic Moment-Based Shallow Water Model for Coupled Bedload–Suspended Load Morphodynamics with Variable Density](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2505.22278v2) | Related erosion/deposition and suspended-load coupling. | The manuscript's rotating exchange model specifies its own simpler two-reservoir closure. |
| [LieSolver: PDE-Constrained Learning for IBVPs via Lie Symmetries](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2510.25731v2), abstract and method | Linear superposition of PDE-constrained solution bricks with appropriate stability for error estimates. The 25 June 2026 revision has this title. | The repository's pinned heat/wave replay tests the original algorithm. Its conclusions do not certify a nonlinear SVE continuation or a cost advantage. |
| [LNN-PINN: A Unified Physics-Only Training Framework with Liquid Residual Blocks](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2508.08935v4), abstract and architecture | Supports attribution of a smooth residual-gating design. | The six-feature SVE benchmark is a compact adaptation. Its four held-out profile results come from this repository. |
| [Estimates on the Generalization Error of Physics-Informed Neural Networks for Approximating PDEs](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2006.16144v3) | Stability enters the step from residual or training error to a solution estimate. | The manuscript states stability and sampling limits separately. |
| [NOWS: Neural Operator Warm Starts for Accelerating Iterative Solvers](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2511.02481v4), [Nemytskii Neural Operator: A Nonlinear Model Reduction Method for Parametrized Partial Differential Equations](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2511.07684v2), and [Understanding and Mitigating Gradient Pathologies in Physics-Informed Neural Networks](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2001.04536v1) | Support the defense discussion of learned initial guesses, online residual correction, and gradient imbalance. | None supplies a matched-accuracy correction-cost result for the dissertation's models. |
| [Solving Ordinary Differential Equations I: Nonstiff Problems](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-540-78862-1), §II.5–II.6 and the DOP853 appendix (checked 27 November 2026 against the table of contents and the [SciPy DOP853 documentation](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.integrate.DOP853.html)) | DOP853 is the Dormand–Prince explicit Runge–Kutta pair of order 8 with embedded error estimators of orders 5 and 3 and a dense-output interpolant; SciPy ports its coefficients. | Used only as a sampled high-precision reference. Its local error control is not a continuous bound; certified numbers come from the interval barrier. |
| [Flow and Bathymetry in Sharp Open-Channel Bends: Experiments and Predictions](https://doi.org/10.1029/2007WR006303), §2 (checked 27 November 2026) | EPFL flume: 193° bend, centreline radius \(R=1.7\) m, width \(B=1.3\) m, vertical banks, \(d_{50}\approx2\) mm. In units of \(R\) the banks lie at \(r\approx0.62\) and \(1.38\), inside the stage-B slice \([0.5,2]\). | Scale only. The flume has secondary circulation, friction and a non-free-vortex inflow; the free-vortex E2 bend does not model it. |

The published rotating experiment photographs shown in the defense are credited to [Dave Rubin and the U.S. Geological Survey](https://www.usgs.gov/media/images/bedforms-produced-three-experiments).
