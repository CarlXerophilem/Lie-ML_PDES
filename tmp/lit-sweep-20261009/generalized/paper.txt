                                                                                  ABSTRACT

                                        Physics-informed neural operators (PINOs) have emerged as powerful tools for learning solution
                                        operators of partial differential equations (PDEs). Recent research has demonstrated that incorporating
                                        Lie point symmetry information can significantly enhance the training efficiency of PINOs, primarily
                                        through techniques like data, architecture, and loss augmentation. In this work, we focus on the latter,
                                        highlighting that point symmetries oftentimes result in no training signal, limiting their effectiveness
                                        in many problems. To address this, we propose a novel loss augmentation strategy that leverages
                                        evolutionary representatives of point symmetries, a specific class of generalized symmetries of the
                                        underlying PDE. These generalized symmetries provide a richer set of generators compared to
                                        standard symmetries, leading to a more informative training signal. We demonstrate that leveraging
                                        evolutionary representatives enhances the performance of neural operators, resulting in improved
                                        data efficiency and accuracy during training.
