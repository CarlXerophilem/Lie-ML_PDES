# Independent review of the frozen 64-candidate conditional search

This review concerns the full two-dimensional cubic Grass SVE equations with
`G=f=0`, `m=3`, `g,K>0`, `h>0`, and `u^2+v^2>0`. It does not concern either
scalar SVE model. The authoritative execution verdict is the separately
generated `nonclassical_review.json`; this account alone is not a release.

## 1. Independent joint-manifold criterion

Write `U=(h,u,v,B)^T`. On the invariant-surface equations `U_y=0` and
`U_t+c U_x=eta`, where `eta=D U+b` and `D=diag(a_h,a_u,a_v,a_B)`, put `p=U_x`.
The original PDE, not an assumed special solution, then requires

```text
M p + eta = 0,   M=A-c I,

    [u  h               0       0]
A = [g  u               0       g]
    [0  0               u       0]
    [0  K(3u^2+v^2)     2Kuv    0].
```

Direct first prolongation of each original two-dimensional equation gives

```text
pr X(E)|Q = D(Mp+eta) + C p,
C=(eta . partial_U) A + A D-D A.
```

Consequently conditional invariance requires `C p=0` for every jet satisfying
`Mp+eta=0`. Checking one reconstructed solution would not establish this.
On `det M!=0` elimination is valid, but does not decide characteristic strata.
Both vector fields commute, have projected rank two everywhere, and prolong
their own invariant-surface equations into their ideal. No extra differential
consequence below first order is omitted from the first-order criterion.

The independent verifier differentiates the four raw two-dimensional PDE
polynomials with respect to fields and first jets. It does not call the
conditional solver's residual or elimination routines.

## 2. Positive certificates and the characteristic counterexample

The zero vertical field and either constant bed shift have identically zero
prolonged PDE residual. These nine frozen distributions remain valid on all
consistent characteristic strata.

For a depth shift `eta=beta e_h`, `c=0`, the only prolonged residual is
`beta u_x`. If `J=Mp+eta`, then the exact identity is

```text
K(3u^2+v^2) beta u_x = beta (J_B-2K v J_v).
```

Its multiplier `K(3u^2+v^2)` is strictly positive on the declared domain; no
division by `u` is needed to prove conditional invariance. At `u=0`, the bed
constraint forces `u_x=0`, whereas mass gives `beta=0`, contradicting the
frozen `beta=+/-1`. This characteristic joint stratum is empty, not an
additional family of solutions or an unexamined successful stratum.

A longitudinal velocity shift `eta=beta e_u`, `c=0`, passes on `u!=0`.
It fails the full domain. Set `g=K=1`, `h=2`, `u=0`, `v=1`, `B=3`, and

```text
(h_x,u_x,v_x,B_x)=(1,0,0,-beta-1),  U_t=beta e_u,  U_y=0.
```

Every original PDE and invariant-surface equation vanishes, while the
prolonged mass equation is `beta!=0`. Hence the two velocity-shift records
must retain `u!=0` as a conditional-invariance guard. A special solution
extending through `u=0` does not repair this failed full-manifold criterion.

The independently predicted partition is 11 full-chart passes, two additional
regular-chart passes, and 51 failures on regular joint jets. For each failure
the verifier uses an explicit rational consistent jet at
`(g,K,h,u,v,B)=(1,1,2,2,1,3)` and records a nonzero prolonged residual.
It also independently clears the regular determinant in the directly
prolonged residual and finds a field-polynomial coefficient whose parameter
monomials all have one nonzero sign. Thus rejection holds for every `g,K>0`,
not just for the rational illustrative parameter pair. A nonzero polynomial
cannot vanish on the open wet/nonzero-speed regular field domain.
The execution receipt determines whether every predicted check actually ran.

## 3. Distribution equivalence is different from solution containment

The classical cubic, nonrotating point field has the general form

```text
(T0+a t) d_t +(X0+b x-r y) d_x +(Y0+b y+r x) d_y
 +2(b-a)h d_h +((b-a)u-r v)d_u +((b-a)v+r u)d_v
 +(Z0+2(b-a)B)d_B.
```

The verifier imposes tangency to the entire candidate distribution and
coefficient-splits the resulting polynomial identities in all point
coordinates. This computes every classical tangent field. Thus a rank-one
intersection excludes a classical generating basis even after an invertible,
coordinate-dependent change of the two generators. Testing membership of
the supplied basis alone would not justify that exclusion.

For the four admitted depth/velocity-shift candidates this ambient
distribution is not generated by a classical rank-two algebra. Nevertheless,
integration of all the reduced equations gives only classical-invariant
solution families:

```text
Depth shift:
 h=H0+beta(t-x/u0), u=u0!=0, v=V0, B=B0+beta x/u0;
 classical generators d_y and d_t+u0 d_x+beta d_B.

Velocity shift on u!=0:
 h=H0, u=U0+beta t, v=V0, B=B0-beta x/g;
 classical generators d_y and d_x-(beta/g)d_B.
```

The original four PDEs and the displayed classical characteristics are
checked independently for both families. The integration also shows
exhaustiveness within these candidate invariant graphs: the bed/transverse
combination first forces `u_x=0`, then the remaining equations fix the
displayed constant or affine fields. All positivity and chart guards remain
local restrictions on these expressions.

No new classically inequivalent solution family follows from this search.
This is a negative novelty result, not a failure of the exact conditional
certificates. The finite registry proves neither a complete nonclassical
classification nor an optimal classification of subalgebras.

## 4. Export and release checks

The verifier reconstructs every affine graph using actual exponentials,
differentiates it directly in the original PDE, and compares both the emitted
pullback and all exponential-polynomial profile coefficients. It checks
numerical encode/decode round trips only as adapter controls, not as PDE or
error certificates. Failed candidates remain diagnostic exports. The two
regular-only candidates require the explicit `u!=0` mask.

`tests/test_stage3_gate.py` uses synthetic isolated records to attack missing
or duplicate candidates, subprocess failure, missing source coverage,
incorrect status counts, falsely promoted diagnostics, erased chart guards,
training flags, stale candidate hashes, omitted proof/reviewer documents,
unbound exports, source drift, and evidence paths outside this cycle.
Synthetic passing receipts are test fixtures and provide no mathematical
evidence for the real candidates.

The candidate runner uses the complete Stage 2 source inventory, including
`cases_2d`. The frozen project-validation runner uses its own inventory, which
omits that directory. The release gate checks each inventory against its own
source enumerator; the candidate run and preceding Stage 2 gate continue to
bind every two-dimensional child source.

The final review receipt binds source/proof files and every candidate/ML JSON
output from `runs/stage3-candidates-final`. Any subsequent edit invalidates
the exact-version release evidence. Project-wide validation and its unchanged
documentary-audit qualification are separate prerequisites for the final gate.
No training, learned-error claim, arbitrary-data well-posedness theorem, or
global continuation theorem is supplied.
