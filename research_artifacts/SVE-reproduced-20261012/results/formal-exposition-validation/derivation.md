# Independent derivation notes for Section 8

Scope: the **printed scalar** equation of Paliathanasis, Leon and Leach, not
the advectively corrected scalar equation or the full bed-field system. Work
locally with smooth point coefficients and
\(g>0,\kappa>0,m\geq1,p=\phi_t>0,q=\phi_s>0\). The positive velocity guard
selects the real branch of \(p^{m-1}\); \(q>0\) keeps the equation defined and
allows its leading jet to be eliminated. No conclusion below covers \(p=0\),
\(q=0\), global solutions, or boundary-data preservation.

## 1. The master equation, line by line

Let \(a=\phi_{tt},b=\phi_{ts},c=\phi_{ss}\) and
\(r=\phi_{ttt},w=\phi_{tts},x=\phi_{tss},y=\phi_{sss}\).
The first current is \(A=qa-gc/q^2\). Commutation of mixed derivatives gives
\(D_tq=b\) and \(D_tc=x\), so
\[
 D_t(qa)=(D_tq)a+q(D_ta)=ba+qr,
\quad D_t(-gc/q^2)=-gx/q^2+2gcb/q^3.
\]
For the second current \(J=m\kappa p^{m-1}b/q\), use
\(D_sp=b\), \(D_sb=x\), and \(D_sq=c\):
\[
D_sJ=m\kappa\left[(m-1)p^{m-2}b^2/q
                   +p^{m-1}x/q-p^{m-1}bc/q^2\right].
\]
Therefore the exact printed residual is
\[
E_m=qr+ab-gx/q^2+2gcb/q^3
-gm\kappa\left[(m-1)p^{m-2}b^2/q
                   +p^{m-1}x/q-p^{m-1}bc/q^2\right]. \tag{1}
\]
Its derivative with respect to \(r\) is \(q>0\), so \(E_m=0\) is equivalent
on this chart to
\[
r=R:=\frac{-ab+gx/q^2-2gcb/q^3
 +gm\kappa\{(m-1)p^{m-2}b^2/q+p^{m-1}x/q-p^{m-1}bc/q^2\}}{q}. \tag{2}
\]
The remaining third jets \(w,x,y\), the second jets \(a,b,c\), and
\(p,q\) are locally free equation-manifold coordinates. This freedom is
the reason coefficient splitting is legitimate; it is not an assertion
about solvability of arbitrary initial-boundary data.

## 2. Why the graph characteristic is Q

For a point transformation generated by
\(X=\tau\partial_t+\xi\partial_s+\eta\partial_\phi\), write to first order
\(T=t+\epsilon\tau\), \(S=s+\epsilon\xi\),
\(\Phi=\phi+\epsilon\eta\). At a *fixed new base point* \((T,S)\), the old
point is \(t=T-\epsilon\tau+O(\epsilon^2)\),
\(s=S-\epsilon\xi+O(\epsilon^2)\). Hence the transformed graph is
\[
\phi^\epsilon(T,S)
=\phi(T,S)+\epsilon(\eta-\tau\phi_t-\xi\phi_s)+O(\epsilon^2).
\]
Thus \(Q=\eta-\tau p-\xi q\) is the infinitesimal *graph* displacement.
An individual graph is invariant if \(Q=0\) on it. This is logically
different from requiring \(\operatorname{pr}^{(3)}X(E_m)=0\) whenever
\(E_m=0\), which asks the transformation to map *all local equation
solutions* to equation solutions.

## 3. Third prolongation without a guessed coefficient form

For any function of the jet variables through order two, the total
derivatives are
\[
D_t=\partial_t+p\partial_\phi+a\partial_p+b\partial_q
 +r\partial_a+w\partial_b+x\partial_c+\cdots,
\qquad
D_s=\partial_s+q\partial_\phi+b\partial_p+c\partial_q
 +w\partial_a+x\partial_b+y\partial_c+\cdots.
\]
The dots indicate higher-jet terms, used only if the differentiated
expression depends on those jets. Set \(\xi^t=\tau,\xi^s=\xi\). Every
prolonged coefficient follows from the recursion
\[
\eta^{\varnothing}=\eta,\qquad
\eta^{Ii}=D_i\eta^I-\phi_{It}D_i\tau-\phi_{Is}D_i\xi,
\quad i\in\{t,s\},\quad |I|\leq2. \tag{3}
\]
In particular,
\[
\begin{aligned}
\eta^t&=D_t\eta-pD_t\tau-qD_t\xi,
 &\eta^s&=D_s\eta-pD_s\tau-qD_s\xi,\\
\eta^{tt}&=D_t\eta^t-aD_t\tau-bD_t\xi,
 &\eta^{ts}&=D_s\eta^t-aD_s\tau-bD_s\xi,\\
\eta^{ss}&=D_s\eta^s-bD_s\tau-cD_s\xi,
 &\eta^{ttt}&=D_t\eta^{tt}-rD_t\tau-wD_t\xi,\\
\eta^{tss}&=D_s\eta^{ts}-wD_s\tau-xD_s\xi.
\end{aligned} \tag{4}
\]
Only these seven coefficients are needed because \(E_m\) contains the
jets \(p,q,a,b,c,r,x\), but not \(w,y\) explicitly. Nevertheless \(w,y\)
enter after prolongation and are free-jets in the determining split.

An economical fully explicit determining expression is
\[
\operatorname{pr}^{(3)}X(E_m)
=E_p\eta^t+E_q\eta^s+b\eta^{tt}+E_b\eta^{ts}
 +E_c\eta^{ss}+q\eta^{ttt}+E_x\eta^{tss}, \tag{5}
\]
where \(T=gm\kappa\) and
\[
\begin{aligned}
E_p&=-T(m-1)\left[(m-2)p^{m-3}b^2/q
 +p^{m-2}x/q-p^{m-2}bc/q^2\right],\\
E_q&=r+2gx/q^3-6gcb/q^4
 +T\left[(m-1)p^{m-2}b^2/q^2+p^{m-1}x/q^2
 -2p^{m-1}bc/q^3\right],\\
E_b&=a+2gc/q^3-T\left[2(m-1)p^{m-2}b/q-p^{m-1}c/q^2\right],\\
E_c&=2gb/q^3+Tp^{m-1}b/q^2,\\
E_x&=-g/q^2-Tp^{m-1}/q.
\end{aligned} \tag{6}
\]
The point coefficients may initially be arbitrary smooth functions of
\((t,s,\phi)\). Substitute (2) in (5) and call the result \(\Omega\).

## 4. Necessary determining coefficients and integration

The following coefficient extraction is sequential: each later row uses
the restrictions proved by previous rows. For \([w]\Omega\) and
\([y]\Omega\), coefficient means the linear coefficient of that independent
jet. For \([ab]\Omega\), it means the mixed derivative with respect to
\(a,b\) after previous restrictions; this avoids ambiguity about informal
polynomial notation.

\[
\begin{array}{c|l|l}
\text{free-jet term} & \text{exact coefficient} & \text{consequence}\\ \hline
y & \displaystyle \frac{g(\kappa mp^mq+p)}{pq^2}(\xi_t+p\xi_\phi)
  & \xi_t=\xi_\phi=0,\ \xi=\xi(s)\\[2mm]
w & \displaystyle \frac{2g(\kappa mp^mq+p)}{pq^2}(\tau_s+q\tau_\phi)
  & \tau_s=\tau_\phi=0,\ \tau=\tau(t)\\[2mm]
ab & -\eta_s/q & \eta_s=0\\
a & q(4p\eta_{\phi\phi}-3\tau''+4\eta_{t\phi})
  & \eta=A(t)\phi+B(t),\ 4A'=3\tau''.
\end{array} \tag{7}
\]
The factors \(p,q,g,\kappa,m\) are positive, and
\(\kappa mp^mq+p>0\). The assertions \(\xi_t=\xi_\phi=0\) and
\(\tau_s=\tau_\phi=0\) also use that \(p,q\) vary on open positive
intervals. The \(a\)-row uses variation of \(p\) to separate
\(\eta_{\phi\phi}\) from \(4\eta_{t\phi}-3\tau''\).

After those consequences, the remaining free-\(x\) coefficient is
\[
[x]\Omega=-\frac{g}{p^2q^2}\left\{\kappa mp^mq
 \left[(m-3)p(A-\tau')+(m-1)(A'\phi+B')\right]
 +p^2(2\tau'+\xi'-3A)\right\}. \tag{8}
\]
The \(q\)-independent and linear-\(q\) parts must vanish separately:
\[
2\tau'+\xi'-3A=0,
\qquad (m-3)p(A-\tau')+(m-1)(A'\phi+B')=0. \tag{9}
\]
Do **not** divide by \(m-1\) or \(m-3\) before splitting strata.

* \(m\ne1,3\): varying \(p,\phi\) in (9) gives
  \(A=\tau'\), \(A'=B'=0\), and \(\xi'=A\). Thus
  \(\tau=\lambda t+t_0,\xi=\lambda s+s_0,\eta=\lambda\phi+u_0\).
* \(m=3\): (9) gives \(A'=B'=0\); (7) gives \(\tau''=0\).
  Write \(\tau=\rho t+t_0,\xi=(3A-2\rho)s+s_0,\eta=A\phi+u_0\).
  This is \(A X_4+(\rho-A)X_5+t_0X_1+s_0X_2+u_0X_3\).
* \(m=1\): (9) gives \(A=\tau'\), while (7) yields
  \(4A'=3\tau''\); since \(A'=\tau''\), both vanish.
  Therefore \(\xi'=A\), but \(B(t)\) is still undetermined.
  Substitution into the **full** residual, not only (8), gives
  \(\operatorname{pr}^{(3)}X(E_1)+2A E_1=bB''+qB'''\).
  Free \(b,q\) imply \(B''=0\), hence \(B=u_0+\nu t\).

Direct off-shell checks close sufficiency:
\(\operatorname{pr}^{(3)}X_{1,2,3}(E_m)=0\),
\(\operatorname{pr}^{(3)}X_4(E_m)=-2E_m\),
\(\operatorname{pr}^{(3)}X_5(E_3)=-E_3\), and
\(\operatorname{pr}^{(3)}X_6(E_1)=0\). The displayed necessary system
and these sufficient identities establish the complete **local smooth
point** algebra for the printed scalar equation on the declared chart.
They do not classify other charts or transformation classes. The bases are
\[
\begin{array}{c|l|c}
m\geq1,\ m\ne1,3 & X_1=\partial_t,\ X_2=\partial_s,
 X_3=\partial_\phi,
 X_4=t\partial_t+s\partial_s+\phi\partial_\phi &4\\
m=3 &X_1,X_2,X_3,X_4,\ X_5=t\partial_t-2s\partial_s&5\\
m=1 &X_1,X_2,X_3,X_4,\ X_6=t\partial_\phi&5.
\end{array} \tag{10}
\]
The \(m=1\) fifth field is missed by the source count. Conversely, the
source's printed determining condition
\(3\eta^1+\xi^2_{,s}+2\xi^1_{,t}=0\) evaluates to \(6=0\) on its
own \(X_4\); use the independently computed split, not that condition.

The exceptional cubic dilation can also be checked by weights. Under
\(X_5=t\partial_t-2s\partial_s\), the jet weights of
\((p,q,a,b,c,r,x)\) are \((-1,2,-2,1,4,-3,3)\).
Every inertial/gravity term in (1) has weight \(-1\), whereas the
bedload divergence \(E_{\rm bed}:=-gm\kappa D_s(p^{m-1}b/q)\)
has weight \(2-m\). Consequently the exact **off-shell** identity is
\[
\operatorname{pr}^{(3)}X_5(E_m)+E_m=(3-m)E_{\rm bed}. \tag{10a}
\]
It vanishes on \(E_m=0\) when \(m=3\); at a generic exponent the
bedload remainder does not vanish identically. For \(X_6=t\partial_\phi\),
the finite transformation adds \(\epsilon t\) to \(\phi\), changing
\(p\) by \(\epsilon\) but leaving \(q,a,b,c,r,x\) unchanged.
At \(m=1\), the bedload current contains \(p^0=1\), so (1) is invariant.

## 5. Similarity and order reduction: separate operations

For \(X=\partial_t+\alpha\partial_s+\alpha_3\partial_\phi\), the
characteristic equation is
\(Q=\alpha_3-\phi_t-\alpha\phi_s=0\). Two invariants of the point
flow are \(z=s-\alpha t\) and \(w=\phi-\alpha_3t\), so an invariant
graph has \(\phi=\alpha_3t+F(z)\). Its jets are
\[
(p,q,a,b,c,r,w_3,x,y)
=(v,P,\alpha^2C,-\alpha C,C,-\alpha^3H,
  \alpha^2H,-\alpha H,H),\quad v=\alpha_3-\alpha P.
\]
Here \(w_3=\phi_{tts}\), avoiding conflict with invariant \(w\).
Since \(D_t=-\alpha D_z\), \(D_s=D_z\) on a profile,
\[
E_m=-\alpha D_z\{B(P)C\},\quad
B(P)=\alpha^2P-g/P^2-gm\kappa v^{m-1}/P. \tag{11}
\]
For \(\alpha\ne0\), integration gives \(B(P)C=I_0\). This first
reduced the **PDE base dimension** from two variables to one via an
invariant graph, then reduced **ODE differential order** by one through an
exact first integral. Solving \(C=I_0/B(P)\) further requires \(B(P)\ne0\).
The \(I_0=0\) affine branch must be retained.

For an admitted scaling, set \(\phi=t^AF(z), z=st^B,t>0\),
\(V=AF+BzP\), and \(D=d/dz+P\partial_F+C\partial_P+H\partial_C\).
Then \(p=t^{A-1}V\), \(q=t^{A+B}P\), and at \(t=1\),
\[
a=(A-1)V+BzD(V),\quad b=D(V),\quad
J_A=Pa-gC/P^2,\quad J_s=m\kappa V^{m-1}b/P. \tag{12}
\]
The inertial/gravity and sediment terms share a time power only if
\(3A+B=2\) and \((m-3)(A-1)=0\). The admitted instances are
\((A,B)=(1,-1)\) for \(X_4\), any \(m\), and
\((A,B)=(0,2)\) for \(X_5\), \(m=3\), plus their cubic mixed
scalings. Their pullback is
\[
E_m[t^AF(st^B)]
=t^{-A-1}\mathcal R_{A,B},\qquad
\mathcal R_{A,B}=-A J_A+BzD(J_A)-gD(J_s). \tag{13}
\]
The coefficient of \(H=F'''\) in \(\mathcal R\) is
\[
L=Bz\left(B^2z^2P-g/P^2-gm\kappa V^{m-1}/P\right). \tag{14}
\]
Besides \(P,V>0\), a regular third-order normal form requires \(L\ne0\).
For \(X_4\), \(V=F-zP\), and the exact derivative identity is
\[
\mathcal R_{1,-1}=D(I_4),\qquad
I_4=C\left(-z^3P+gz/P^2
 +gm\kappa z(F-zP)^{m-1}/P\right). \tag{15}
\]
Thus \(I_4=I_0\) is an order-lowering integral, separate from the
similarity quotient. An exact power-law profile may have \(L=0\) along
it; do not insert that profile into the divided normal form.

The numerical comparison is evidence about chosen dimensionless local IVPs,
not a theorem about global accuracy or the paper's physical data. Likewise,
the graph and determining calculations are authored/SymPy-audited here, not
automatic discovery by a general PDE classifier.
