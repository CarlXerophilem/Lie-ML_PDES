## Chat limitation

The supplied context exposes seven project session titles with truncated message
excerpts. They are saved separately in `chats/`, together with this export
request. Retrieved historical summaries are saved separately and labeled.
There is no accessible native project-history export in the current interfaces.
Consequently **this is not a complete transcript export of all original chats**.
Missing assistant turns, omitted sessions, exact later session identities and
unavailable image/attachment associations require the native account export.
No reconstructed dialogue is labeled as original.

## Bibliography and source status

Title/author metadata for supplied PDFs comes from the files themselves where
readable. Primary web records were checked for the Nature Communications flow
paper, LieSolver v2 and PMLR TM-PINN. Other existing references retain their
manuscript provenance and unresolved metadata notes. The supplied Navier--Stokes
PDF identifies its author as OpenAI but does not establish a public publication
record or a validated theorem. Unidentified book-page scans are preserved
without an invented book attribution.

Additional public PDFs were fetched where endpoints returned actual PDF bytes.
`references/public-download-status.json` reports their result. Every unavailable
download retains its bibliographic URL; an endpoint failure is not a missing
invented PDF. Inventory-only citations are clearly marked and are not used as
scientific evidence.

## Validation scope

`validation/VALIDATION.md` records fresh checks, archived evidence and omissions
separately. Neural training and the original repository regression suite were
not rerun merely to package the project. Archived checkpoints/metrics are
preserved with hashes. A successful export is not a new research-result claim.
