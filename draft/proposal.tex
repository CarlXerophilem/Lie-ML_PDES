\section{Research Problem}
The starting point is the printed Lagrangian master equation of Paliathanasis, Leon and Leach \cite{lagrange}:
\begin{equation}
\E_m[\phi]:=
\partial_t\left(\phi_s\phi_{tt}-g\frac{\phi_{ss}}{\phi_s^2}\right)
-g\beta\partial_s\left(\phi_t^{m-1}\frac{\phi_{ts}}{\phi_s}\right)=0.
\label{eq:master}
\end{equation}
Here $\phi(t,s)$ is fluid-particle position, $u=\phi_t>0$ and $h=1/\phi_s>0$. Fix $(m,g,\beta)=(3,1,0.1)$. The source's cubic symmetry gives
\[
X_5=t\partial_t-2s\partial_s,\qquad \phi(t,s)=F(\sigma),\quad \sigma=st^2.
\]
The specific problem is to approximate the reduced profile IVP on $I=[1,3/2]$:
\begin{equation}
a(\sigma,F')F'''+b(\sigma,F',F'')=0,\qquad
(F,F',F'')(1)=\left(K,\frac K3,-\frac{2K}{9}(1+\delta)\right),
\label{eq:problem}
\end{equation}
where $K=\sqrt[3]{45}$ and $\delta=0,0.01$. The coefficients are given below. This varies profile curvature data, not the flux exponent. Compare Taylor, numerical and neural approximations by profile error, residual and cost.

\section{Objectives}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Verify the reduction and retain the printed-model reconstruction audit.
\item Derive a conditional regular-branch residual-to-profile error bound.
\item Test two Taylor--PINN trials against exact and classical numerical controls.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Methodology}
\subsection{Model Scope and Source Verification}
Equation~\eqref{eq:master} is the research target, not an assumed equivalent form of the physical Eulerian system. The source uses $\beta=mA_g\xi$ for bedload flux $A_gu^m$ and porosity factor $\xi$. Only the selected cubic symmetry and reduction are needed; a complete Lie classification is outside scope.

For the usual bed pullback $\widetilde B(t,s)=B(t,\phi(t,s))$,
\[
\partial_x=\phi_s^{-1}\partial_s,\qquad
(\partial_t)_x=(\partial_t)_s-\frac{\phi_t}{\phi_s}\partial_s.
\]
Consequently the Eulerian Exner equation transforms to
\begin{equation}
\widetilde B_t-\frac{\phi_t}{\phi_s}\widetilde B_s+
\beta\phi_t^{m-1}\frac{\phi_{ts}}{\phi_s}=0.
\label{eq:physical}
\end{equation}
The transport term is absent from the source's printed Equation (6). Define
\[
A=\phi_s\phi_{tt}-g\phi_{ss}/\phi_s^2,\qquad
q=\phi_t^{m-1}\phi_{ts}/\phi_s.
\]
The printed pair is $\widetilde B_s=-A/g$, $\widetilde B_t=-\beta q$.
Its compatibility condition $A_t-g\beta q_s=0$ is precisely the master equation. On a simply connected regular region this reconstructs a printed-system bed up to a constant, but does not establish \eqref{eq:physical}.

The publisher PDF has been checked against the supplied file. Adopted invariants, the power-law control and the working ODE are verified by direct calculation. The full manuscript records proofs and an exact counterexample to unrestricted Eulerian equivalence. Physical-model correction is a separate project, not a prerequisite to this study.

\subsection{Reduction and Conditional Error Bound}
The characteristic invariant of $X_5$ is $\sigma=st^2$ \cite{olver}. With $r=F'$ and $v=F''$, direct substitution gives
\begin{equation}
\E_3[F(st^2)]=t^{-1}R[F],\qquad R[F]=aF'''+b,
\label{eq:lift}
\end{equation}
where
\[
\begin{aligned}
a&=8(1-g\beta)\sigma^3r-2g\sigma/r^2,\\
b&=4\sigma(1-4g\beta)r^2+8\sigma^2(3-5g\beta)rv\\
 &\quad+[8\sigma^3(1-g\beta)+4g\sigma/r^3]v^2.
\end{aligned}
\]
Require $r>0$ and $a\ne0$. Then $Y=(F,r,v)$ satisfies the regular ODE
\[
Y'=G(\sigma,Y)=(r,v,f),\qquad f=-b/a.
\]
For the fixed parameters, exact rational interval arithmetic verifies
\[
|a|\ge\frac{24217}{7225},\qquad \|D_YG\|_\infty\le15
\]
on $I\times\mathcal B$, with
$\mathcal B=[17/20,5/4]\times[-17/20,-7/20]$ for $(r,v)$.

\textbf{Conditional theorem.} Let a $C^3$ trial $\widehat F$ match the three initial data. Suppose $(\widehat F',\widehat F'')$ stays inside $\mathcal B$ with uniform boundary margin $\eta>0$, and
\[
\sup_I|\widehat F'''-f(\sigma,\widehat F',\widehat F'')|\le\varepsilon,
\qquad
\Delta=\frac{\varepsilon}{15}(e^{15/2}-1)<\eta.
\]
Then the exact reduced IVP exists on $I$ and
$\|\widehat Y-Y\|_{\infty,I}\le\Delta$. A Gronwall estimate and first-exit argument prove the statement. Uniform residual and margin bounds are required; samples do not suffice. A trained-network certificate is a stretch outcome, not an assumed result.

On $\Omega=\{(t,s):1\le t\le5/4,\ st^2\in I\}$, the exact lift also gives
$\|\E_3\|_{\infty,\Omega}\le\|R\|_{\infty,I}$. This is not an Eulerian solution-error theorem.

\subsection{Taylor--PINN Construction and Comparison}
Let $z=\sigma-1$. Compute
\[
P_2=F_*+r_*z+\tfrac12v_*z^2,\qquad
P_4=P_2+\tfrac16 f_*z^3+
\tfrac1{24}(f_\sigma+f_rv+f_vf)_*z^4.
\]
The subscript $*$ denotes evaluation at the initial state. Compare
\[
\widehat F_{\theta,N}=P_N(z)+z^{N+1}n_\theta(4z-1),\qquad N=2,4.
\]
Both trials enforce the same three initial data. Higher $N$ changes the Taylor information and remainder factor, so an advantage cannot be attributed to Taylor order alone. Taylor--neural remainders are established prior art \cite{taylor}; our scalar third-order implementation is not their full state-system method.

Use two 16-unit tanh hidden layers and a scalar output, totaling 321 parameters. Train without solution labels using the PINN loss \cite{pinn}
\[
\mathcal L_N(\theta)=\frac1{128}\sum_{j=1}^{128}
\left[\widehat F_{\theta,N}'''(\sigma_j)+
\frac{b(\sigma_j,\widehat F_{\theta,N}',\widehat F_{\theta,N}'')}
{a(\sigma_j,\widehat F_{\theta,N}')}\right]^2.
\]
Differentiate the single output, check raw $R$ as well as $R/a$, and mark sampled $F'\le0$, $|a|<10^{-6}$ or nonfinite values as failed runs.

For each $\delta$, compare $P_2/P_4$, DOP853 and both neural trials using seeds $0,1,2$. Use 128 training points and 1001 distinct evaluation midpoints. Fix 5000 Adam steps, then at most 200 L-BFGS iterations, on float64 CPU. Report every seed, function evaluations, derivative errors, residuals, regularity margins and training/evaluation cost.

Symmetry-informed losses and transformed collocation have prior art \cite{liepinn,shah}. Compare against a practically tuned numerical baseline at matched accuracy \cite{baselines}; neither literature nor a low training loss establishes neural superiority.

\subsection{Feasibility and Eight-Week Schedule}
The source's exact cubic family supplies
\[
F_0=K\sigma^{1/3},\qquad
K^3=\frac{27g}{1-4g\beta}=45,\qquad
a(\sigma,F_0')=2K\sigma^{7/3}>0.
\]
The master residual and arbitrary-profile lift are symbolically checked. On 501 points, the classical exact-control state error is $\LagrangeControlError$. For the perturbed data, refinement differs by $\LagrangeRefinement$ and Radau by $\LagrangeOracle$. These are sampled diagnostics, not interval-certified solution errors.

Preparatory CPU tests on 12 September 2026 ran both neural trials with one seed and 400 Adam steps. Profile errors were about $1.09\times10^{-5}$ for $N=2$ and $1.09\times10^{-4}$ for $N=4$; normalized residuals were about $6.39\times10^{-2}$ and $1.32\times10^{-2}$. The rankings disagree. These runs establish bounded feasibility, not a formal comparison or a certified network.

The mathematical focus is one reduced IVP, not general PDE solving. The two-month undergraduate study in \textbf{2027} is scheduled as:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Weeks 1--2: source audit, coordinates, symmetry and reduction.
\item Weeks 3--4: Taylor profiles, conditional bound and numerical controls.
\item Weeks 5--6: declared multi-seed neural experiments and cost measurements.
\item Weeks 7--8: error--cost analysis, reproducibility checks and final writing.
\end{enumerate}
No arbitrary boundary data, shocks, new symmetry classification or physical-model correction is promised. Exact calendar dates remain to be set.
