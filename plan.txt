# Research Plan — Semi-Analytical & Machine-Learning Methods for Solving PDEs via Lie Symmetry Analysis

> **Goal.** Reduce complex PDEs into simpler forms via Lie symmetry analysis and obtain exact or
> approximate analytical solutions, with applications in mathematical physics, chemistry, and
> engineering — fusing classical group analysis, semi-analytical series methods, and machine learning.

# PART I — THE RESEARCH PLAN

## 0. Thesis & novelty in one paragraph

**CCS-SYM+ — a Convergence-Certified, Symmetry-Reduced, Semi-Analytical/ML solver.** We make *rigorous,
falsifiable error control on Lie-reduced PDEs* — not raw accuracy — the headline contribution. A target PDE
`Δ=0` is (i) **reduced** by its Lie point (or, where point symmetry is barren, nonclassical/approximate)
symmetry to a low-dimensional ODE `N[U]=0`; (ii) **closed** by a homotopy-analysis (HAM) deformation series
carrying a convergence-control parameter `ħ`; (iii) **certified** at runtime by a *theorem-backed* three-part
certificate (a conditional convergence-ratio test, a closedness lemma so convergence implies a true solution,
and a computable a-posteriori error bound); and (iv) **corrected/globalized** by a *small* symmetry-invariant
neural network expressed in invariant coordinates, trained only where a generator actually carries signal.
The novel artifact none of the prior lineages provides: **a per-problem, runtime, theorem-backed
convergence-AND-error certificate fused with (approximate-)Lie reduction and an interpretable ML remainder,
validated only against closed-form exact similarity solutions on a laptop CPU.**

**Why this is heuristically new (not a relabel).** Each ingredient exists in isolation; *the certified fusion
does not*. Concretely:

- **vs. the approximate-homotopy-symmetry lineage** (Jiao–Gao–Lou 2009; Liu 2010): they first built homotopy
  *into* approximate-symmetry reduction, but produce formal series with `ħ` tuned by eye, **no convergence
  proof**, and (Kawahara) no numerical validation. CCS-SYM+ adds the runtime certificate `ρ_k ≤ r < 1` *on the
  reduced equation* plus an a-posteriori residual/error bound — exactly what that lineage lacks.
- **vs. Turkyilmazoglu (2010) / Liao (2009):** HAM convergence is proven only *conditionally* on a ratio bound
  that must be checked numerically per problem; Liao himself lists a-priori convergence as open. Nobody has
  **operationalized** that bound as a cheap certificate on a *Lie-reduced* (hence low-dimensional, more likely
  convergent) equation. That operationalization is the core.
- **vs. PI-OHAM (Uddin 2026):** replaces manual `ħ`-tuning with a PINN-style residual loss — but on *one ODE*
  (Blasius), with **no PDE, no Lie symmetry, no convergence proof**. We add the symmetry front end, multiple
  PDEs, and the certificate.
- **vs. symmetry-loss PINN/PINO** (Akhound-Sadegh, NeurIPS 2023; "Generalized Lie Symmetries in PINOs",
  arXiv:2502.00373 — both in local `ref/`): these prove Lie *point*-symmetry losses "oftentimes result in no
  training signal" (for Burgers all prolonged generators reduce to `0` or `c·Δ`) and propose evolutionary
  representatives, but the Sobolev mechanism is explicitly a hypothesis and tests cover only Burgers/Darcy.
  **CCS-SYM+ does not compete as another symmetry-loss method**: it uses symmetry as a *coordinate/reduction
  device + ground-truth supplier*, invokes generators (evolutionary/nonclassical) only where they are known to
  give signal, and — via the **dead-generator-as-design-principle** lemma — makes that failure mode
  *structurally absent* in invariant coordinates instead of penalizing a zero-signal term.
- **vs. weak-baseline ML-PDE work:** McGreivy–Hakim (Nat. Mach. Intell. 2024) found 79% of ML-for-PDE papers
  use weak baselines; Grossmann et al. (2024) show FEM often beats PINNs. We validate against **closed-form
  exact solutions** (Cole–Hopf tanh, Barenblatt, Ablowitz–Zeppetella, Blasius `f''(0)=0.332057`) and run a
  **matched-wall-clock honesty study** that reports negatives.

## 1. Background & positioning

### 1.1 Classical Lie machinery is mature but unevenly automated
The pipeline *generator → prolongation → determining equations → Lie algebra → optimal system → reduction →
invariant solutions + conservation laws* is algorithmic and well covered by CAS (Maple `PDEtools`/GeM/SADE/
DESOLV; Mathematica `SYM`/MathLie). Two steps stay hard: (a) integrating large overdetermined determining
systems (documented cross-package incompleteness; no standard correctness benchmark), and (b) optimal-system
construction (only fully automated for 3D/4D real Lie algebras as of Amata–Oliveri–Sgroi 2025; manual for
higher/infinite-dim algebras). Open-source Python tooling is immature.

### 1.2 Beyond-classical reductions reach solutions point symmetry misses — but the payoff is equation-dependent
Nonclassical/Q-conditional symmetries (Bluman–Cole; Clarkson–Kruskal) and conditionally-integrable
reaction-diffusion families (Cherniha–Davydovych; Broadbridge et al. 2023–24) yield extra reductions for
Burgers/Boussinesq/FitzHugh–Nagumo — but add **nothing** for KdV. Approximate (Baikov–Gazizov–Ibragimov, BGI)
symmetries are rigorous but software-poor; Tarayrah–Pitzel–Cheviakov (2023) prove that *unstable* BGI point
symmetries of an unperturbed PDE need not survive perturbation (unlike ODEs) — motivating a **stability
screen**.

### 1.3 Semi-analytical series: HAM is theoretically strongest, but convergence is conditional
HAM's convergence-control parameter `ħ` is the real advance over ADM/HPM/VIM (which lack it; HPM is the `ħ=−1`
case). The cleanest theory (Turkyilmazoglu 2010) is conditional: *if* successive terms satisfy a ratio bound
`r<1` in a Banach space, *then* the series converges to the exact solution — checkable only numerically per
problem. Optimal-`ħ` via residual minimization is CPU-expensive at high order. The 2022–26 frontier is
fractional Laplace-RPSM and ML hybridization (PI-OHAM).

### 1.4 The Lie + semi-analytical combination exists but is shallow
Three weakly-connected clusters: the genuine fusion (approximate-homotopy-symmetry, unproven convergence);
the loose "reduce-by-similarity-then-HAM" pattern (provable only in the *fractional* case via Erdélyi–Kober
power series); and ML embeddings (Invariant-Net 2026). **No head-to-head benchmarking of HAM vs ADM vs DTM vs
power series on the same Lie-reduced ODE exists**, and no work closes a Lie-reduced ODE with a neural solver
*inside* a classical reduction pipeline.

### 1.5 Symmetry-informed ML has a documented central failure mode
Mechanisms: data augmentation (Brandstetter 2022, ×4–×8 on KdV/KS), loss augmentation (Akhound-Sadegh 2023;
Zhang SPINN/ISC, JCP 2023), equivariant architectures (G-FNO for compact groups; LieLAC for non-compact at
5–30× inference cost), canonicalization. The verified critique: standard point-symmetry losses give *no
training signal* on key cases. ML symmetry *discovery* (LieGAN; symmetry-constrained SINDy/PDE-FIND; Yang et
al. 2025 differential-invariant libraries) mostly recovers global/affine groups, not a specific PDE's full
point algebra from data.

### 1.6 Honest overall picture (and references to re-confirm)
The field is over-claimed: weak baselines are common and toy 1-D/2-D constant-coefficient PDEs dominate, while
high-D, stiff/multiscale, fractional, and chemistry/engineering PDEs — exactly where analytical reduction
should pay most — are under-tested. **Confirmed in local `ref/`:** Akhound-Sadegh (NeurIPS 2023);
"Generalized Lie Symmetries in PINOs" (arXiv:2502.00373); FNO (Li et al.); "Lie Algebra Canonicalization"
(arXiv:2410.02698). **From the web sweep, re-confirm bibliography before citing:** Turkyilmazoglu (2010);
Liao (2009); Jiao–Gao–Lou (2009); Liu (2010); Uddin PI-OHAM (2026); McGreivy–Hakim (Nat. Mach. Intell. 2024);
Grossmann et al. (2024); Tarayrah–Pitzel–Cheviakov (2023); Cherniha–Davydovych; Broadbridge et al. (2023–24);
Amata–Oliveri–Sgroi (2025); Yang et al. (2025).

## 2. Core methodology & the key mathematics

**Notation.** Scalar PDE `Δ(x,t,u^{(k)})=0`; generator `X = τ ∂_t + ξ ∂_x + φ ∂_u`; k-th prolongation
`pr^{(k)}X = X + Σ_J φ^J ∂_{u_J}`, with `φ^J = D_J(φ − τ u_t − ξ u_x) + τ u_{tJ} + ξ u_{xJ}` (`D_·` total derivative).

**(S0) Discover — invariance / determining system.** The symmetry condition is
`pr^{(k)}X(Δ)|_{Δ=0} = 0`. Splitting it on free jet monomials yields a *linear, overdetermined* determining
system whose null space is the algebra. *Nonclassical:* append the invariant-surface condition
`Q := φ − τ u_t − ξ u_x = 0` before splitting (a *nonlinear* Q-conditional system). *BGI approximate
(perturbed `Δ = Δ_0 + ε Δ_1`):* set `X = X_0 + ε X_1`, impose `pr X(Δ) = O(ε²)`; the `O(1)` order gives `X_0`,
`O(ε)` the stable deformation `X_1`; **stability screen** keeps `X_0` iff a completing `X_1` exists.

**(S1) Reduce.** Invariants from `dt/τ = dx/ξ = du/φ` give the similarity variable `z` and field `U`;
substitution yields the reduced operator `N[U]=0`, verified by back-substitution (residual 0). Examples
(all verified, Part II): Burgers travelling wave `z = x − ct` ⇒ `ν U'' = (U−c) U'`; porous medium
`u = t^{−α} F(η), η = x t^{−β}, β = 1/(m+2)` ⇒ `(F^m F')' + β (η F)' = 0`; Fisher–KPP ⇒ `U'' + c U' + U(1−U) = 0`.

**(S2) Close semi-analytically (HAM).** Deformation `(1−q)L[φ−u_0] = q ħ H N[φ]`, `φ = Σ_m u_m q^m`; the
m-th order linear problem `L[u_m − χ_m u_{m−1}] = ħ H R_m`, with `R_m` the `(m−1)`-th `q`-derivative of
`N[Σ u_i q^i]` at `q=0` and `χ_m = 0 (m≤1), 1 (m>1)`. Output `U_M(z;ħ) = Σ_{m=0}^M u_m(z)` — interpretable.

**(S3) Certify (the upgraded core, three parts).**
- *(a) Ratio / convergence:* `ρ_k = ‖u_{k+1}‖/‖u_k‖` in L²/sup norm; report a flag in
  `{CERTIFIED-conditional-on-monotone-tail, INCONCLUSIVE, DIVERGENT}`. Geometric-tail bound: if
  `ρ_k ≤ r < 1` for `k ≥ k_0`, then `‖U − U_M‖ ≤ ‖u_{k_0}‖ r^{M−k_0+1}/(1−r)`.
- *(b) Closedness lemma (R1):* if `N` is Lipschitz on the iterate ball and `{U_M}` is Cauchy, then
  `U_M → U_∞` with `N[U_∞] = lim N[U_M] = 0` — so series convergence *provably* gives a solution of the
  reduced equation (closes the logical gap a naïve ratio test leaves open).
- *(c) Computable error bound (R2):* write `N = L + 𝒩` (`L` the reduced linear part); if `‖L^{-1}‖ ≤ C_L`,
  `𝒩` Lipschitz with constant `𝓛`, and `C_L 𝓛 < 1`, then
  `‖U − U_M‖ ≤ (C_L /(1 − C_L 𝓛)) ‖N[U_M]‖ =: C ‖N[U_M]‖` — an *actual* a-posteriori error bound with `C`
  computed from `L` and a Lipschitz estimate. Optimal `ħ* = argmin_ħ ‖N[U_M(·;ħ)]‖₂`.

**(S4) Correct + globalize (symmetry-invariant ML).** Ansatz `u_θ(x,t) = Λ(z)·N_θ(z) + U_M(z;ħ*)` with `z`
the invariant chart and `Λ` the known scaling prefactor: *exactly* equivariant under the reduction subgroup at
machine precision, **no loss term, no inference-time canonicalization**. Loss
`J(θ) = w_r‖N[u_θ]‖²_coll + w_bc‖B[u_θ]‖² + w_sym‖(X_0+εX_1)(u_θ)‖²` (approximate-ISC penalty, nonzero on the
*real* equation). **Dead-generator lemma:** a point generator whose evolutionary representative gives
`pr v_Q(Δ) ≡ 0` contributes no signal *and* its orbit is exactly the one the invariant chart quotients out —
so the pathology is structurally absent here, not penalized.

## 3. Phased plan (each phase: objective → tasks → tools → deliverable → falsifiable gate)

> Gates are numeric pass/fail with an explicit *honest negative-result branch*. Phases 0–3 are de-risked by
> the verified computations in Part II; Phases 4–6 extend them.

### Phase 0 — Foundations & knowledge base  *(largely complete)*
- **Objective.** Reproducible CPU environment; a reusable prolongation engine; curated literature base.
- **Tasks.** Pin `sympy 1.14 / numpy 2.3 / scipy 1.17 / torch 2.12+cpu`; consolidate the `ref/` → `pdf2md`
  corpus (Part III); lock the jet-space engine (`prolong2`, `invariance_residual`).
- **Deliverable.** `symm/feasibility_demo.py` (+ output); annotated bibliography (§1.6).
- **Gate.** Engine verifies a known generator (residual 0) and rejects a non-symmetry (residual ≠ 0).
  ✅ **Met** (Burgers ×5, Fisher-KPP ×3 + negative controls).

### Phase 1 — Symmetry engine: auto-discovery, reduction, optimal systems  (S0+S1; milestone M1)
- **Objective.** Move from *verifying* hand-supplied generators to *solving* the determining system and
  emitting reductions automatically.
- **Tasks.** Generic polynomial infinitesimal ansatz → build `pr^{(k)}X` → on-shell substitute highest
  derivative → split into the linear determining system → `sp.solve` for the null space. Commutator/adjoint
  tables → minimal optimal system of 1-D subalgebras → invariants → reduced ODE; verify by back-substitution.
- **Tools.** `sympy`; optional Maple `PDEtools`/SADE hand-off for systems / `(2+1)`-D.
- **Deliverable.** `symm/symmetry_engine.py` returning `(algebra, optimal_system, reduced_ODEs)`.
- **Gate (M1).** From scratch, auto-recover the Burgers algebra (**dim 5**) and the porous-medium scaling
  family, and emit residual-0 reductions. *De-risked:* a degree-≤2 ansatz already yields 28 split determining
  equations whose unique branch has exactly 5 free parameters. **Fail-branch:** hand off to Maple and document
  the `sympy` limitation.

### Phase 2 — Semi-analytical closure + the convergence/error certificate  (S2+S3; theoretical core)
- **Objective.** Genuine HAM deformation series on the reduced ODE + the theorem-backed three-part certificate.
- **Tasks.** Implement HAM `L^{-1}` recurrence (symbolic); cross-check with residual power series; compute
  `ρ_k` (L²/sup), the geometric-tail bound, and the `{CERTIFIED/INCONCLUSIVE/DIVERGENT}` flag; **prove** the
  closedness lemma (R1) and derive the computable constant `C` (R2) per benchmark; `ħ*` by bounded
  `scipy.optimize.minimize_scalar`.
- **Tools.** `sympy`, `numpy`/`scipy`.
- **Deliverable.** `symm/ham_certify.py`; a short theory note (R1/R2 statements + per-benchmark constants).
- **Gate (M2a).** On the **non-integrable** Fisher-KPP reduced ODE: produce a genuine HAM series whose `ρ_k`
  is demonstrably `ħ`-dependent (certifies for some `ħ`, diverges for others) **and** show `C·‖N[U_M]‖`
  *brackets* the true error vs the exact AZ wave. *De-risked:* `ρ_k` already diverges at `ħ=−1`, certifies at
  `ħ=−0.3/−0.6`; order-wise rel-L2 1.0e-1 → 4.5e-2 → 2.0e-2. **Fail-branch:** if `C_L 𝓛 ≥ 1`, downgrade to an
  honestly-labeled residual *diagnostic* + the geometric-tail bound; never present a vacuous bound.

### Phase 3 — Symmetry-invariant ML correction  (S4; milestones M2b, M3)
- **Objective.** A small CPU PINN that corrects/globalizes the certified series in invariant coordinates.
- **Tasks.** Implement `u_θ = Λ(z) N_θ(z) + U_M(z;ħ*)`; boundary-anchored ansatz for exact BCs; train by
  L-BFGS on a few hundred collocation points; report the bounded-corrector norm `‖N_θ‖/‖U_M‖`; use the
  evolutionary/nonclassical generator where point symmetry is dead.
- **Tools.** raw `torch` (CPU); `matplotlib`.
- **Deliverable.** `symm/pinn_corrector.py`; learned-`ħ` Blasius script.
- **Gate (M2b/M3).** Fisher-KPP corrector reaches **< 1e-3 rel-L2** vs exact AZ wave on CPU in seconds;
  Blasius recovers **`f''(0)=0.332057` to 4 digits** with a learned `ħ` + attached certificate.
  *De-risked:* a 321-param MLP already gives rel-L2 **8.7e-4** in **0.13 s**.

### Phase 4 — Approximate & nonclassical symmetry for *perturbed, real* PDEs  (graft G1; BGI + stability screen)
- **Objective.** Extend beyond exactly-symmetric idealizations to near-symmetric physical/chemical/engineering
  PDEs, with correct symmetry information for the *real* equation.
- **Tasks.** BGI order-by-order determining solve; Tarayrah–Pitzel–Cheviakov stability screen as an
  admissibility gate; singular-perturbation detection (degenerate leading operator → matched asymptotics with
  inner rescaling); nonclassical fallback restricted to families where it pays (Burgers, reaction-diffusion).
- **Tools.** `sympy` (`solve`/`groebner`); the inner/outer asymptotic backbone.
- **Deliverable.** `symm/bgi_pipeline.py`.
- **Gate.** On perturbed Burgers `u_t + u u_x = ε u_xx`, recover the inner boundary-layer kink and screen out
  unstable generators. *De-risked:* inner-layer kink residual already 0 (`bgi_probe.py`). **Negative result is
  publishable:** report which generators fail the stability screen.

### Phase 5 — Benchmark campaign + the McGreivy–Hakim honesty study  (milestone M4)
- **Objective.** Validate across physics/chemistry/engineering against exact oracles; settle whether symmetry
  is a *fundamental* advantage or a *data proxy*.
- **Tasks.** Run the full suite (§4); the only head-to-head **HAM vs RPS on the same Lie-reduced ODE**;
  matched-wall-clock comparison of CCS-SYM+ vs (i) plain PINN with equal extra collocation, (ii) Lie data
  augmentation, (iii) broken exact-symmetry loss, (iv) tuned spectral/FEM.
- **Tools.** the full stack; closed-form oracles (Cole–Hopf, Barenblatt, Ablowitz–Zeppetella, Blasius).
- **Deliverable.** reproducible benchmark tables + figures (`ρ_k` decay, certificate-vs-true-error bracketing,
  equivariance gap, time-to-accuracy) — **including negatives**.
- **Gate (M4).** Every solution ships the three-part certificate; the honesty study reports time-to-accuracy
  honestly. If symmetry reduction does *not* beat extra collocation, that is reported (and reframes the
  headline as *certificate + interpretability*, not speed).

### Phase 6 — Fractional / variable-coefficient frontier + write-up  (milestone M5, gated)
- **Objective.** Supply the missing HAM-on-Erdélyi–Kober-reduced-ODE convergence reporting; first
  symmetry-informed-ML benchmark in this regime.
- **Tasks.** Time-fractional / variable-coefficient KdV reduction → fractional ODE with proven power-series
  truth → certificate vs analytic truth. Gated on Phases 1–3 (sympy fractional calculus is immature).
- **Deliverable.** fractional benchmark + manuscript with the contributions of §7.
- **Gate (M5).** Certificate flags match the analytic power-series convergence behavior. **Gating rule:** do
  not start until M1–M3 pass.

## 5. Evaluation: baselines & metrics

**Baselines.** plain PINN with equal extra collocation (matched wall-clock — the proxy-vs-fundamental test);
Lie data-augmentation PINN; PINN + broken exact-symmetry loss (dead-generator baseline); tuned spectral/FEM;
pure HAM/OHAM truncation (ML-ablation); RPS on the *same* reduced ODE; PI-OHAM on Blasius; **closed-form exact
similarity solutions as the ground-truth oracle** (never self-generated FDM).

**Metrics.** rel-L²/L∞ vs the *exact* solution (Fisher-KPP < 1e-3; Blasius `f''(0)` to 4 digits);
**certificate tightness** = (a-posteriori bound)/(true error), must bracket; **certificate calibration** =
fraction of `{CERTIFIED/INCONCLUSIVE/DIVERGENT}` flags matching reality; `ρ_k` sequence + fitted `r` per `ħ`;
**equivariance gap** (target ~1e-15 vs O(1) for raw-coordinate PINN); bounded-corrector norm `‖N_θ‖/‖U_M‖ ≪ 1`;
wall-clock-to-accuracy (matched); symmetry-discovery success (recovered algebra dim vs known); BGI
stability-screen outcomes; HAM/RPS term count + symbolic-complexity growth (handoff order).

## 6. Risks & mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Closedness lemma (R1) needs `N` Lipschitz; fails near degeneracies (porous-medium free boundary, fractional). | Prove on a weighted/interior subdomain; let ML handle the boundary; report scope honestly. |
| Computable constant (R2) needs `C_L 𝓛 < 1`; vacuous for strong nonlinearity. | Report `C` and the product; when ≥1 downgrade to a labeled *diagnostic* + geometric-tail bound. |
| Ratio test is conditional & finite-data; can't guarantee the infinite tail. | Three honest flags + independent error bound; never say "guarantee" for the infinite tail. |
| `sympy` may stall on systems / `(2+1)`-D / fractional. | Scope to scalar `(1+1)` low order where it provably works; Maple/SADE hand-off; gate on M1. |
| BGI generator instability for some perturbations. | Screened in S0; report as a finding; pick benchmarks with a known stable generator. |
| Symbolic HAM term blow-up + costly high-order `ħ*`. | Cap order modestly (order-6 cheap, verified); offload remainder to PINN; cheap 1-D `ħ*` search. |
| McGreivy–Hakim critique rebounding (no speed win on easy 1-D). | Position headline as *certificate + interpretability*, not speed; report negatives. |
| Integrative-novelty critique (each piece is prior art). | Deliver the **theorems** (R1 closedness, R2 constant, dead-generator equivalence) so the contribution is theorem-level, not synthesis. |

## 8. End-to-end workflow (pseudocode)

```text
CCS-SYM+(PDE Δ, optional perturbation ε·Δ1, BCs/ICs):
  # S0 DISCOVER  ──────────────────────────────────────────────
  build pr^(k)X via jet engine;  split pr^(k)X(Δ)|_{Δ=0}=0 → determining system
  algebra ← sp.solve(determining system)                       # linear, overdetermined
  if perturbed:  X=X0+εX1; solve O(1),O(ε); STABILITY-SCREEN(X0)   # keep iff X1 completes
  if point algebra barren:  append Q=φ−τu_t−ξu_x; solve nonclassical (Q-conditional)
  # S1 REDUCE  ────────────────────────────────────────────────
  optimal_system ← commutator/adjoint(algebra)
  for generator in optimal_system:
      z,U ← invariants(dt/τ=dx/ξ=du/φ);  N[U] ← substitute(Δ, z,U)
      assert back_substitution_residual(N[U]) == 0
  # S2 CLOSE  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
  U_M(z;ħ) ← HAM_deformation_series(N[U], order M)             # + RPS cross-check
  # S3 CERTIFY  ───────────────────────────────────────────────
  ρ_k ← ‖u_{k+1}‖/‖u_k‖;  flag ∈ {CERTIFIED, INCONCLUSIVE, DIVERGENT}
  verify Lipschitz(N) → closedness lemma (R1)                  # convergence ⇒ N[U_∞]=0
  C ← C_L/(1−C_L·𝓛);  error_bound ← C·‖N[U_M]‖                 # R2 (if C_L·𝓛<1, else DIAGNOSTIC)
  ħ* ← argmin_ħ ‖N[U_M(·;ħ)]‖₂
  # S4 CORRECT + GLOBALIZE  ───────────────────────────────────
  u_θ = Λ(z)·N_θ(z) + U_M(z;ħ*)                                # exact equivariance, small MLP
  minimize  w_r‖N[u_θ]‖²_coll + w_bc‖B[u_θ]‖² + w_sym‖(X0+εX1)(u_θ)‖²   (L-BFGS, CPU)
  return  u_θ,  {flag, ρ_k, error_bound, ħ*, ‖N_θ‖/‖U_M‖},  error-vs-exact-oracle
```

# PART II — VERIFIED FEASIBILITY (already run on this laptop CPU)

All scripts live in `symm/`; all results below were executed and checked (sympy 1.14 / numpy 2.3 /
scipy 1.17 / torch 2.12+cpu), total runtime seconds-scale, no GPU.

**`feasibility_demo.py`** (Burgers + porous medium + series + `ħ*`):
- Burgers `u_t+u u_x=ν u_xx`: 5 generators (`∂_t, ∂_x`, Galilean `t∂_x+∂_u`, scaling `2t∂_t+x∂_x−u∂_u`,
  projective `t²∂_t+tx∂_x+(x−tu)∂_u`) all give **invariance residual ≡ 0**.
- Travelling wave `z=x−ct` ⇒ `ν U'=½(U−c−k)(U−c+k)`; tanh kink `U=c−k tanh(k z/2ν)` residual **0**.
- Porous medium `u_t=(u^m u_x)_x`: scaling symmetry **iff** `a=2b−m e`; Barenblatt
  `F=(C−(mβ/2)η²)^{1/m}, β=1/(m+2)` first integral residual **0** for `m=1,2,3`.
- 8-term recurrence series reproduces the exact Taylor of `1−tanh z` (max err **1.56e-2** on [−1,1]);
  residual-minimizing convergence parameter `h*` recovers exact `k/2ν` to **1.7e-7**.

**`fisher_kpp_reduction.py`** (primary non-integrable testbed — the headline certificate):
- Symmetries `∂_t, ∂_x, ∂_t+c∂_x` residual **0**; scaling negative control correctly `u(u−2)≠0`.
- Reduction to `U''+cU'+U(1−U)=0` verified; exact Ablowitz–Zeppetella `U=1/(1+B e^{z/√6})²` residual **0**.
- **Genuine HAM deformation series** in bounded `s=e^{z/√6}`: Turkyilmazoglu ratios are **`ħ`-dependent** —
  `ħ=−1` diverges mid-tail (`ρ_k` crosses 1: 1.105, 1.175), `ħ=−0.6/−0.3` certify (all `ρ_k<1`). Order-wise
  rel-L2 vs exact: **1.02e-1 → 4.48e-2 → 2.04e-2** (M=3,5,7). Independent `solve_ivp` cross-check matches to
  **3.6e-10** in the well-posed direction (forward integration is the known Fisher-front saddle, not a bug).
- **⇒ the convergence certificate is a real, falsifiable, non-tautological object.**

**`pinn_corrector_demo.py`** (Stage S4, the ML leg): a **321-parameter** MLP, boundary-anchored so BCs are
exact, trained by L-BFGS on 400 CPU collocation points in **0.13 s**, reaches **rel-L2 = 8.7e-4** vs the exact
AZ front (residual RMS 3.0e-4) — meeting the < 1e-3 target.

**`bgi_probe.py`** (Phase 4 seed): perturbed Burgers `u_t+u u_x=ε u_xx` reduces to the singular-perturbation
ODE; the inner-layer kink `W=c−k tanh(kZ/2)` has residual **0** — the outer/inner split is the analytic
backbone for the BGI leg.
